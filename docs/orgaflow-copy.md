## 4. Features

**Section header:** Everything your team needs, nothing it doesn't

### Estimates & Invoices
Build professional quotes with line items, send a secure link, and get a one-click approval — no client login required. Convert any approved estimate to an invoice instantly, branded PDF included.
- Client approves from a shareable link
- Estimate → invoice in one click
- PDF export with your logo and colors

### Customer CRM
Every client in one place: history, activity, and every linked estimate and invoice — scoped to your workspace. Know exactly where each relationship stands before you pick up the phone.

### Automations + Tasks
Here's where Orgaflow pulls ahead: the moment a client pays an invoice or approves an estimate, Orgaflow creates the task and assigns it to the right person. No manual handoffs, no dropped balls.
- Triggers like *invoice paid* and *estimate approved*
- Auto-create tasks with title, assignee, and due date
- Track everything on a visual Kanban board
- Customize stages to match how your team actually works

### Payments & Collections
Log payments manually or let clients pay online via Stripe. See what's collected, outstanding, and overdue — per invoice, per client, per period — at a glance.
- Online payments with Stripe Connect *(Scale plan)*
- Invoice status updates itself when fully paid
- Know your real cash position without a spreadsheet

### Reports & Analytics
Revenue by period, outstanding balances, quote-to-win conversion — the numbers that actually run a small business, no accounting degree required.

## 8. FAQ

**How is Orgaflow different from FreshBooks or QuickBooks?**
Those are accounting tools. Orgaflow is built around the estimate-to-payment-to-delivery workflow — quoting, invoicing, payments, and the tasks that follow, in one place, without the accounting overhead.

**Can clients approve estimates without an account?**
Yes. Every estimate gets a secure shareable link where clients review and approve in one click — no signup required.

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes. Starter is free and gives solo operators the core estimate, invoice, customer, and item workflow, with usage limits.

**Do you support multiple currencies?**
Yes. Set a default currency for your workspace and per-client currencies for international work.

**Is this good for solo freelancers?**
Absolutely. Starter is built for one-person operations, and Orgaflow scales as your team grows.

**Can I import my existing clients and invoices?**
Yes — add clients individually or in bulk. Most teams are fully set up in under five minutes.

**How do online payments work?**
Connect Stripe (Scale plan) and clients pay invoices online. Status updates automatically when an invoice is fully paid.
